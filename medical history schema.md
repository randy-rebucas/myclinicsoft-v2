A medical history schema is a structured format or framework used to gather and organize information about a patient's past and present health status, as well as relevant medical events and conditions. It typically includes various categories of information such as:

1. **Demographic Information:** This includes the patient's name, age, gender, contact information, and other relevant demographic details.

2. **Chief Complaint (CC):** The primary reason the patient is seeking medical attention.

3. **Present Illness (PI):** A detailed description of the current health issue, including its onset, duration, severity, associated symptoms, and any factors that exacerbate or alleviate it.

4. **Past Medical History (PMH):** Information about past medical conditions, surgeries, hospitalizations, and significant illnesses the patient has experienced.

5. **Medication History:** A list of current medications, including prescription drugs, over-the-counter medications, supplements, and herbal remedies. It may also include information about medication allergies or adverse reactions.

6. **Family History (FH):** Details about the medical conditions and health history of the patient's family members, especially close relatives such as parents, siblings, and children.

7. **Social History (SH):** Information about the patient's lifestyle, habits, occupation, marital status, living environment, and relevant social factors that may impact their health.

8. **Allergies:** Any known allergies to medications, foods, environmental factors, or other substances.

9. **Immunization History:** A record of the patient's immunizations, including vaccinations received and dates administered.

10. **Review of Systems (ROS):** A systematic inquiry about the patient's current symptoms and health status across various body systems, such as cardiovascular, respiratory, gastrointestinal, neurological, musculoskeletal, etc.

11. **Physical Examination (PE):** Findings from the patient's physical examination, including vital signs (e.g., blood pressure, heart rate, temperature), general appearance, and specific physical findings related to the presenting complaint.

12. **Diagnostic Tests and Results:** Information about any diagnostic tests or procedures performed, along with their results.

Using a structured medical history schema helps healthcare providers gather comprehensive and organized information about patients, which is essential for making accurate diagnoses, developing treatment plans, and providing appropriate care.
